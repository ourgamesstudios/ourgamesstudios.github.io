Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

1. Who we are

Our Flower Garden (the "Game") is provided by Muhammed Baha Karadağlı ("we", "us"), the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. Contact: ourgamesstudios@gmail.com.

2. Data we process

  • Account data: your email address (if you sign in with email), the identifier and email address shared by Apple or Google when you sign in with them, and a user ID created by Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile and game data: the player name and farm name you choose, your character, garden, house, inventory, gold and gems, orders, market listings, Lab results, leaderboard scores, and letters you send to your farm partner.
  • Purchase data: records of in-app purchases (product, transaction ID, date). Payments are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your card details.
  • Device and usage data: language, time zone, notification token, app version and in-game events (for example "flower planted"), collected through Firebase Analytics.
  • Advertising data: when ads are shown, Google AdMob may process device identifiers and approximate location (derived from your IP address) to deliver and measure ads, according to the choices you make in the consent form.

3. Why we use it

  • To create your account and run the Game, including shared farms, the market and leaderboards (performance of our agreement with you).
  • To deliver purchases and prevent fraud and cheating (agreement, legitimate interest and legal obligations).
  • To send game notifications you allowed (your consent; you can turn them off in your device settings).
  • To understand how the Game is played and improve it (legitimate interest, or consent where required).
  • To show ads; ads are personalised only if you consent (consent).

4. What other players can see

Your player name, farm name, character, garden showcase, market stall and leaderboard position can be seen by other players. Letters can be seen only by the members of your farm. Please do not put personal information in names or letters.

5. Service providers and transfers abroad

We use Google Firebase (sign-in, database, cloud functions, analytics), Google AdMob (ads), RevenueCat (purchase verification), Expo (push notifications), Apple and Google (sign-in and payments) and Branch (invite links). These providers may store data on servers outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. Where the law requires it (for example Article 9 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and the GDPR), these transfers rely on standard contractual clauses or other permitted safeguards. We do not sell your personal data.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account (Profile → Delete Account), your account and profile data are deleted; if you are the last member of a farm, the farm and its letters are deleted too. Purchase records may be kept for as long as needed for fraud prevention, accounting and legal obligations. Analytics and advertising providers keep data according to their own retention settings.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Article 11 of the KVKK in Türkiye or the GDPR in the EU/EEA), you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw your consent at any time. You can change your ad choices from Profile → Ad Privacy (when shown) and delete your account in the Game. For any other request, email ourgamesstudios@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye: the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK).

8. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it. Where local law sets a higher age for consent, users below that age need a parent's permission.

9. Security

We use access-controlled databases, server-side checks for purchases and encrypted connections. No system is completely secure; if a data breach affects you, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will show the new date here and tell you in the Game about significant changes.

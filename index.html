What you'll do

A whole little world to grow

Every day there's something new to plant, sell, unlock or decorate.

Plant, water, harvest

Buy seeds, dig the soil, fertilise and water your plots, then harvest fresh flowers before they wilt.

  • Grow tulips, roses, sunflowers and many more
  • Level up to unlock rarer seeds
  • Fertilise for bigger, faster harvests

Make your customers smile

Customers line up at your counter with bouquets in mind. Fill their orders on time to earn gold and XP.

  • Customers with their own wishes and moods
  • Special VIP and atelier orders
  • Sell extra flowers to other players at the market

Decorate your home

Furnish a cosy house room by room and make it yours, then visit your neighbours' farms for inspiration.

  • Beds, sofas, plants, rugs and more
  • Bigger houses as you progress
  • Visit other players' gardens

Dress up your farmer

Pick hair, outfits and accessories to create your character, and match looks with your partner.

  • Hair, hats, tops, shorts and shoes
  • New outfits to unlock in the shop
